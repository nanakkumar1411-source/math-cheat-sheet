1 · GRAPHS

Rate is slope, total is height

Straight line = same rate. Flat = rate 0. A corner or curve = the rate changed.
  • Steeper after a corner: rate went up
  • Flatter after a corner: rate went down

2 · WHAT A NUMBER MEANS

Plain number = value at 0

The number next to x is the change for each 1 more.
A = 10d + 471
471 → the start. 10 → the gain per step.
a0 = 1, so f(0) = 1 + b

3 · PERCENT

Turn it into a multiplier

  • +13% → ×1.13, and −16% → ×0.84
  • "1.13 times" means 13% greater (subtract 1)
  • Two changes: multiply the multipliers
  • Going backward: divide, don't subtract
+167%, then −16%: 2.67 × 0.84 = 2.2428 → 124.28%

5 · WORDS TO EQUATION

Count what the total counts

"30 sides" means add how many of each. Don't multiply by lengths.
n + 6 + 5n = 30 → 6n + 6 = 30

6 · SYSTEMS

Is one equation a multiple of the other?

  • Both sides multiple: same line, infinitely many solutions
  • Left side multiple, right side not: parallel, no solution
  • Neither: they cross once
8x + 7y = 9 and 24x + 21y = 27
(second is 3 × first) → same line
You missed: Practice 1 Q21.
7 · INEQUALITIES

Negative? Flip the sign.

−3x ≤ 12 → x ≥ −4
ax ≤ 56 and x ≥ −8 → a is negative
56a = −8 → a = −7
Then plug −7 back in to check.

8 · CHUNKS

Don't solve for x

3x2 − 18x − 15 = 0, find x2 − 6x
÷ 3: x2 − 6x − 5 = 0 → x2 − 6x = 5
Rearrange until the chunk sits alone.

R = 7r, H = 8h → 72 × 8 = 392
